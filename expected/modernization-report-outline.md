# Expected Discovery Report Shape

## Executive summary

Short summary of readiness, major blockers, and recommendation to continue or pause.

## Clear requirements

List what is already specified well enough to proceed.

## Missing requirements

List unresolved business, technical, and operational requirements.

## Risks and dependencies

Call out cross-team, runtime, and integration risks.

## Recommended next actions

Provide a short prioritized next-step list before design starts.
